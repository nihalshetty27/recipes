Instant Ramen

Instant Ramen is a precooked, dried noodle block sold with flavoring powder, seasoning oil, or a dried vegetable packet that cooks in a few minutes with boiling waters.

Ingredients

  • 1 tablespoon vegetable oil
  • 1 yellow onion, diced
  • 5 cloves garlic, thinly sliced
  • 1 (1 inch) knob fresh ginger, minced
  • 1 bunch green onions, chopped, white and green parts separated
  • 6 to 8 cups water
  • 3 packages ramen, seasoning packets separated, divided
  • 3 cups rotisserie chicken, shredded into large pieces, or more to taste
  • 2 tablespoons soy sauce
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 2 tablespoons soy sauce
  • 1 jalapeno, thinly sliced, for garnish
  • chopped fresh cilantro, for garnish
  • Sriracha, to taste, for garnish

Steps

  1. Pour vegetable oil into a large stock pot over medium-high heat. Add yellow onion and white part of green onion and cook until softened, about 3 minutes.

  2. Add garlic and ginger; cook and stir until fragrant, about 30 seconds.

  3. Add water, seasoning packets and chicken to the stock pot, bring to a boil, and reduce heat; simmer for 5 minutes. Add soy sauce and ramen noodles to the stock pot; simmer until noodles are tender, about 4 minutes, stirring occasionally.

  4. Serve with jalapenos, green part of green onions, cilantro, and Sriracha.
